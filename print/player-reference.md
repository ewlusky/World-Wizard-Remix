# World Wizard Remix — Player Reference

*One sheet. Everything else is lookup.*

---

## Your turn

**1. Pick your path.** 🎡 **Spin the wheel** and do what it lands on, or 🎯 **Choose** any action.

**2. Roll or decide.** Roll on the table, pick from it, or **make something up.** All three are always legal.

**3. Pay the AP.** **4. Draw it on the map** — an outline and a label is enough. **5. Write one line in the Log.** **6. +1 to the Tally.**

**You may skip your turn.** No cost, no reason owed.

---

## The four Ages

| Age | AP | Tally | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| I. Primordial | 1d6+3 | 16 | Land forms, magic sources appear |
| II. Prehistoric | 2d6 | 14 | Peoples emerge, legends begin |
| III. Ancient | 2d6 | 12 | Civilisations rise, magic gets institutions |
| IV. Present | 2d6 | 10 | Recent history. No new terrain — the land is set |

Roll AP once per Age. When the Tally hits its number and everyone agrees, run the Survival Round.

---

## The four ways the economy bends

**🪞 Complicate** — *gain +1 AP.* Give something you made a flaw, contradiction, mystery, internal rift, limitation, or private sorrow. Once per creation per Age. Write it down.

**💥 Pass** — *gain +1d6 AP,* +1 per 2 things you control. Then roll Disasters **and** Disaster Scope. **It lands on your own stuff:** pick 4 of your creations it could plausibly hit, roll 1d4. Describe it in detail. Bold the hex.

**⭐ Weave Bonus** — use or connect to another Steward's creation, with their agreement. The +1 surcharge vanishes *and they get +1 AP.* Star the hex. Stars stack, no cap.

**🌙 Quiet Turn** — *free, no AP, no Tally, any time.* Add a detail, connect two things in one sentence, or sketch something small on an existing hex. **A complete turn, not a lesser one.**

---

## Stewardship

Whoever made it owns it. Changing someone else's thing costs **+1 AP** — unless you ask and they agree, which makes it a Weave instead. You do not have to steward well.

---

## Ending an Age — Survival Round

Roll **2d6** per People and Avatar you Steward. Spend AP for **+2 each**.

| 2d6 | |
|---|---|
| **10–12** | **Thrives.** People +1 Level and a free City next Age. Avatar becomes immortal. |
| **7–9** | **In danger.** Push the roll, or survive *changed* — you describe how. |
| **2–6** | **Fades.** One **final legendary act**, then gone. The marks stay on the map. |

**Magic between Ages:** Wellsprings and Nexuses persist. Breaches roll 2d6 — 2–4 widens, 5–9 stable, 10–12 seals to a Thin Place. Bound spirits persist. Unbound follow Avatar rules.

---

## War — 2d6 when you expand into occupied hexes

| 2d6 | | Who narrates the cost |
|---|---|---|
| 10–12 | Aggressor takes the hexes | — |
| 7–9 | Pyrrhic — aggressor takes them | **Defender** |
| 4–6 | Defender holds, at great cost | **Aggressor** |
| 2–3 | Defender repels handily | **Defender** |

**Invest:** 1 AP shifts the roll ±1, declared before rolling. Note that the winner never narrates their own triumph.

## Levels & Alignment

**Nascent** 1d6 hexes · **Developing** 2d6−1 · **Advanced** 2d6. A Group Endeavor advances one Level, so does surviving an Age.

Alignment: Good / Lawful / Neutral / Chaotic / Evil. It's a vibe, not a contract. Shifts one step at a time.

---

## 📜 Language

Words go on the **Compendium**, a page kept separate from the map: *the word · what it means · who said it first.*

**Coin a Word** 1 · **Gift a Name** 1 (name someone *else's* thing in your tongue — the best cooperative move in the game) · **Assign a Tongue** 1 · **Sound-Shift** 1 (old form stays on the map, both are canon) · **Take a Loanword** *gain +1* · **Invent Writing** 2 (a written tongue can never Sound-Shift again) · **Let a Tongue Die** *gain +2* (only when its last People Fades).

No mechanical reward for saying the words out loud. Say them if you like saying them.
