# Age IV: Present

> *Recent history. The stage is set, and something is about to happen on it.*

**AP: 2d6.** Roll once for the Age. **Tally: 10.**

**You can no longer create new terrain. The land is set.** Everything in this Age happens to things that already exist. The shortest Age, and the one that hands the world to whoever plays in it next.

## Notes for this Age

**Uncover Artifact is the Age's thesis.** Nothing is being made from nothing any more. The interesting move is finding what earlier Ages left behind and asking what it means now. If your table only has one turn left, spend it here.

**Quiet Turns matter most in this Age.** Ten Tally is short and the map is crowded. Adding a detail or drawing a connection between two things that have never touched is often worth more than another Avatar.

## Ending the game

When the Tally reaches 10 and you are ready to finish, run [the Survival Round](05-ending-an-age.md) one last time. Then sit with the map and talk through four questions:

- What area would make the best setting for adventures?
- What mysteries remain unsolved?
- What conflicts are brewing?
- What stories are waiting to be told?

The answers are the handoff. This game exists to produce a place worth playing in, and this conversation is where you find out what you made.

## Porting note

Restructured into the icon families used from Age II onward, keeping the source's Essence / Divine / Veil split. ARTIFACTS is kept as its own group because the source treated it separately and it is the thematic centre of the Age. No action, cost, or effect was changed.
