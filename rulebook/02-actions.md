# Actions

Four families. Pick one, spend the AP, make the thing.

Table references point at [`../tables/`](../tables/). Rolling is always optional — pick an entry, or invent.

## 🌍 MAKE LAND

| Action | AP | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Grow Terrain | 2 | Roll 1d6; add that many hexes of one terrain. [Table 1: Mundane Terrain](../tables/01-mundane-terrain.md) or pick. |
| Add a Feature | 1 | A single dramatic landmark, 1 to 10 hexes. [Table 3: Dramatic Features](../tables/03-dramatic-features.md) or pick. |
| Enchant a Feature | 1 (+1†) | Take something already on the map and make it magical. [Table 2: Magical Terrain](../tables/02-magical-terrain.md) for the twist. |

## 🧬 MAKE LIFE

| Action | AP | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Create a People | 2 | A sentient species emerges. [Table 4: Peoples](../tables/04-peoples.md) or invent. Name them, pick a home hex, pick an Alignment. They start **Nascent**. |
| Create an Avatar | 1 | A legendary being walks into the story. [Table 5: Avatars](../tables/05-avatars.md) or invent. Give them a name and where they are found. |
| Create a Creature or Spirit | 1 | One notable being: beast, essence-creature, spirit, ghost. [Table 7](../tables/07-significant-species.md) or [Table 13](../tables/13-significant-beings.md) or invent. Remarkable, but not sentient like a People. |
| Expand Territory | 1 (+1†) | A People spreads. Roll expansion by their Level (see [reference](04-reference.md#people-development-levels)). Draw the new claimed hexes. |
| A Deed | 1 (+1†) | An Avatar does something that matters. Pick a verb, describe what and where and who it touches. See [Deeds](04-reference.md#deeds). |
| Group Endeavor | 2 (+1†) | A People does a thing *together*: founds a settlement-site, forms a pact with an Avatar, takes up worship of a Spark, attunes to a Wellspring, becomes a faction. Any "they come together around X." Advances their Level. |

## ✨ MAKE MAGIC

| Action | AP | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Invest a Vessel | 2 | A Spark pours part of itself into a creature, Avatar, or place. That thing gains a piece of the Spark's Domain. |
| Open a Breach | 2 | Tear a new Thin Place open, or widen one that exists. Something from Beyond the Veil can cross. Roll for what slips through. |

## 💥 OTHER

| Action | AP | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Complicate | **gain +1** | Make one of your own creations more interesting. Once per creation per Age. See [Complicate](03-the-ap-economy.md#complicate). |
| Pass | **gain +1d6** | Get AP now, and the world bites back. See [The Price of Passing](03-the-ap-economy.md#the-price-of-passing). |

---

† **Touching someone else's work.** Actions marked **(+1†)** cost 1 extra AP when they change something another Steward owns.

But read [The Weave Bonus](03-the-ap-economy.md#the-weave-bonus) before you treat that as a tax. If the other Steward agrees to the action, the surcharge disappears *and* they get paid. The dagger is not a wall around other people's things; it is a prompt to go ask them.
