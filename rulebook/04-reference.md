# Reference

Read only when you need it.

## Stewardship

Whoever made a thing is its **Steward**. You decide what happens to it, unless another Steward spends the extra AP to change it. Stewardship can be shared, by agreement, or handed on as a gift.

You do not have to steward well. Peoples can fall into ruin. Avatars can betray each other. That is the fun part.

## People Development Levels

| Level | Expansion roll | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nascent | 1d6 hexes | Just emerged |
| Developing | 2d6 − 1 hexes | Growing society |
| Advanced | 2d6 hexes | Reached in the Ancient Age |

A Group Endeavor advances a People one Level. So does surviving an Age — see [ending an Age](05-ending-an-age.md).

## Alignment

Peoples, Avatars and Factions pick one when created: **Good / Lawful / Neutral / Chaotic / Evil.**

Do not take it seriously. It is a vibe. It can shift one step at a time when something in the story changes them.

## War

When Expand Territory moves into another People's hexes, roll 2d6.

| 2d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 10–12 | Aggressor wins and claims the hexes. |
| 7–9 | Pyrrhic victory. Aggressor takes them, but the defender describes the cost. |
| 4–6 | Defender keeps the hexes, at great cost. The aggressor decides what that cost is. |
| 2–3 | Defender repels the attack handily. They name what it cost the aggressor. |

**Invest.** Either side may spend AP to shift the roll, 1 AP for ±1, declared before the roll.

Note who describes the cost in each band. The winner never gets to narrate their own triumph unopposed, and a losing side always gets to say what the loss meant. That is where the history comes from.

## Deeds

Verbs to pick from when an Avatar acts:

Awaken · Banish · Bless · Curse · Challenge · Conquer · Create · Defend · Destroy · Discover · Guide · Heal · Inspire · Liberate · Mourn · Name · Negotiate · Overthrow · Sacrifice · Seduce · Transform · Trick · Unite · Witness

These are examples, not a list. A Deed can be anything you think qualifies as one.

## Currents and Nexuses

**Currents** are ley lines. Where two or more cross, that is a **Nexus**.

Nexuses are active and powerful. Anything built on one is amplified: cheaper Deeds, cheaper Endeavors, stronger magic. Mark a Nexus every time a new Current passes through an existing one.

Currents and Nexuses carry across Ages.
