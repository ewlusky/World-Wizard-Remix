# Age I: Primordial

> *Millions of years. The raw world takes shape. There is no one here to see it yet.*

**AP: 1d6 + 3.** Roll once for the Age. **Tally: 16.**

Geography forms and the sources of magic appear. Nothing sentient exists yet, so every action in this Age is about the land and the power running under it.

## Actions

| Family | Action | AP | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🌍 **MAKE LAND** | Create Mountain Range | 1 | Draw a mountain chain, a cluster, or isolated peaks. |
| 🌍 **MAKE LAND** | Create River System | 1 | Draw a river from source to sea. It may branch. |
| 🌍 **MAKE LAND** | Create Feature | 1 | A dramatic landmark, 1 to 10 hexes. [Table 3: Dramatic Features](../tables/03-dramatic-features.md). |
| 🌍 **MAKE LAND** | Create Terrain | 2 | Roll 1d6; add that many areas of one terrain type. [Table 1: Mundane Terrain](../tables/01-mundane-terrain.md). |
| 🌍 **MAKE LAND** | Create Magical Terrain | 2 | Roll 1d6; add that many areas of supernatural terrain. [Table 2: Magical Terrain](../tables/02-magical-terrain.md). |
| ✨ **MAKE MAGIC** | Create Wellspring | 1 | Place a font of raw magical energy. [Table 6: Sources of Magic](../tables/06-sources-of-magic.md), or the legacy [Wellspring Types](../tables/supplementary.md#wellspring-types). |
| ✨ **MAKE MAGIC** | Draw Current | 1 | Connect two Wellsprings with a ley line. Where Currents cross, mark a **Nexus**. |
| ✨ **MAKE MAGIC** | Thin the Veil | 1 | Create a place where another realm presses close. Roll [Beyond the Veil](../tables/supplementary.md#beyond-the-veil) for what is on the other side. |
| ✨ **MAKE MAGIC** | Place Spark | 2 | A fragment of divine power enters the world. Roll [Spark Domains](../tables/supplementary.md#spark-domains). |
| 💥 **OTHER** | Pass | **+1d6** | Gain AP, then pay [the Price of Passing](03-the-ap-economy.md#the-price-of-passing). |

Everything in [the AP economy](03-the-ap-economy.md) applies in every Age, including the Quiet Turn, the Weave Bonus, and Complicate. Those are not Age-specific and are not repeated in the Age tables.

## Notes for this Age

**There is nothing alive to complicate yet.** Complicate works on anything you Steward, which in this Age means terrain, features, Wellsprings and Sparks. A mountain range with a contradiction in it is a perfectly good complication, and doing it early pays off for the rest of the game.

**Currents are infrastructure.** Every Current drawn now is something a later People can settle on, fight over, or lose. Nexuses in particular make everything built on them cheaper for the rest of the game, so where you cross your ley lines in Age I decides where civilization happens in Age III.

**The Survival Round does not apply.** There are no Peoples or Avatars to resolve. When the Tally hits 16 and everyone agrees, move to Age II.

## Porting note

Restructured into the icon families used from Age II onward. The source listed these ten actions in a flat table; the grouping is the refactor the family edition's own design notes said should be applied to the remaining Ages. No action, cost, or effect was changed.
