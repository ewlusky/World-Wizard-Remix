# Ending an Age — the Survival Round

When the Tally reaches its target and everyone agrees it is time, resolve every **Avatar** and **People** created this Age, plus any older ones still around.

The Steward rolls 2d6 per creation. You may spend AP to boost a roll, **+2 per AP**.

| 2d6 | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 10–12 | **Thrives.** A People advances one Level and may found one free City next Age. An Avatar becomes *immortal* and survives into the next Age unchanged. |
| 7–9 | **In danger.** Spend AP to push the roll up, or they survive *changed* — the Steward describes how they were diminished, scattered, or transformed. |
| 2–6 | **Fades.** The Steward gets **one final legendary act**: a free Deed, a last Group Endeavor, a final migration, a lasting curse. Then they pass from the world. Their marks stay on the map. |

## On fading

A failed roll is not deletion. The creation gets a last move, and it should be a good one — the point is that things leave the world the way things actually leave the world, with a final gesture that outlives them.

Anything drawn on the map stays drawn. Ruins are content.

## Magic between Ages

| What | What happens |
|---|---|
| Wellsprings and Nexuses | Persist. *Optional:* roll 1d6 each — on a 1 it weakens, on a 6 it strengthens, otherwise unchanged. |
| Breaches | Roll 2d6 each. 2–4 widens, mark it. 5–9 stable. 10–12 seals into a Thin Place, mark it differently. |
| Bound Spirits | Persist. No roll. |
| Unbound Spirits | Follow the Avatar survival rules above. |

## Then

Start the next Age. Roll new AP, reset the Tally, and keep the map.
