# Age III: Ancient

> *Thousands of years. Civilizations rise, and magic stops being weather and starts being institutions.*

**AP: 2d6.** Roll once for the Age. **Tally: 12.**

The land is mostly settled, so terrain gets expensive and new Peoples get rare. What is cheap now is *organisation*: factions, orders, structures, artifacts. This is the Age where the world grows a politics.

## Notes for this Age

**Terrain at 3 AP is a statement, not a tax.** If you are still reshaping continents in the Ancient Age, that should cost you a whole turn's worth of everything else. Enchanting or complicating land that already exists is almost always the better buy.

**Sealing a Breach is the first purely defensive action in the game.** It makes something *not* happen. That is worth noticing: by this Age the world has enough in it that protecting a thing is a real move.

**Ending the Age.** When the Tally hits 12 and everyone agrees, run [the Survival Round](05-ending-an-age.md).
