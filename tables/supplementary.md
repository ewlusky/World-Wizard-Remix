# Supplementary Tables

Small, action-specific tables that the [main fifteen](README.md) do not cover. These come from the earlier ruleset and were kept because each one is the only answer to a question an action actually asks.

Roll d100, or pick.

---

## Wellspring Types

*What kind of raw magical font is this?* Used by **Create Wellspring** ([Age I](../rulebook/07-age-i-primordial.md)).

The 100-entry [Table 6: Sources of Magic](06-sources-of-magic.md) supersedes this for detail. This shorter list is kept because ten broad types are easier to work with when you are placing the world's very first magic and want room left to define it later.

| Roll | Type | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| 01-10 | Primal Fire | Eternal flames, heat, transformation |
| 11-20 | Deep Waters | Mystical springs, tidal pools, underwater fonts |
| 21-30 | Living Stone | Crystal formations, singing rocks |
| 31-40 | Wild Growth | Impossible vegetation, life-giving energy |
| 41-50 | Storm Heart | Crackling lightning, wind, thunderous power |
| 51-60 | Shadow Deep | Tangible darkness, cold, silence |
| 61-70 | Star Fall | Cosmic, otherworldly energy from above |
| 71-80 | Dream Font | Blurs reality, causes visions |
| 81-90 | Time Eddy | Temporal distortions; past and future bleed |
| 91-100 | Chaos Spring | Unstable, ever-changing, unpredictable |

## Beyond the Veil

*What is on the other side of this thin place?* Used by **Thin the Veil** ([Age I](../rulebook/07-age-i-primordial.md)), **Open a Breach** ([Age II](../rulebook/02-actions.md)), and **Planar Incursion** ([Age IV](../rulebook/09-age-iv-present.md)).

| Roll | The Beyond | What dwells there |
|---|---|---|
| 01-12 | Spirit Wild | Nature spirits, animal souls, primal forces |
| 13-24 | The Dreaming | Dream beings, living nightmares, wish-creatures |
| 25-36 | Gray Lands | The dead, ghosts, ancestral spirits, memories |
| 37-48 | Fey Reaches | Faeries, tricksters, ancient bargainers |
| 49-60 | Elemental Deep | Pure elemental beings — living fire, water, earth, air |
| 61-72 | Shadow Mirror | Dark reflections, doppelgängers |
| 73-84 | Celestial Heights | Angelic beings, cosmic order |
| 85-96 | Infernal Depths | Demons, tempters, corruptors |
| 97-100 | Far Realm | Alien minds, impossible geometries, madness |

Whatever a Breach opens onto stays open until someone spends AP to **Seal** it. Roll this once per Breach and write it down; a Breach with a known other side is far more useful than a generic hole.

## Disaster Scope

*How much of the world did that just affect?* Rolled alongside [Table 9: Disasters](09-disasters.md) every time someone pays [the Price of Passing](../rulebook/03-the-ap-economy.md#the-price-of-passing).

| Roll | Scope | Area affected |
|---|---|---|
| 01-40 | Limited / Local | About 1d10+1 hexes |
| 41-90 | Wide / Regional | About 2d10+10 hexes |
| 91-99 | Continental | About 3d10×10 hexes |
| 100 | Global | The entire world |

A rolled 100 changes the world for everyone, not just the Steward who Passed. Play it out at the table rather than writing it alone.

## Magical Catastrophes

*Magic went very wrong.* Used by **Magical Catastrophe** ([Age IV](../rulebook/09-age-iv-present.md)).

| Roll | Catastrophe | Consequences |
|---|---|---|
| 01-14 | Wellspring Detonation | A source of Essence explodes, devastating the area |
| 15-28 | Spark Death | A divine Spark is destroyed, orphaning its followers |
| 29-42 | Veil Collapse | A large region loses its barrier; the Beyond floods in |
| 43-56 | Artifact Awakening | A powerful artifact activates uncontrollably |
| 57-70 | Divine War | Multiple Sparks enter direct conflict |
| 71-84 | Magic Death | Magic stops working entirely in a region |
| 85-100 | The Unraveling | Reality itself begins to come apart |

Every one of these destroys something a player made, which is the point. Before rolling, be sure the table is in the mood for it.
