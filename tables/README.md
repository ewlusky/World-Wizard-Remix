# Tables

**15 tables, 100 entries each, 1,500 entries total.** Every one is d100-rollable, and every one is also just a list you can read and pick from.

Ported 2026-08-09 from `LatestWorldWizardTables.docx`, the master document behind the printed binder. This is the largest single asset in the project.

## How to use them

Rolling is never mandatory. In order of preference at the table:

1. **Spin the wheel** to find out *which* table, if you want the choice narrowed. See [WHEEL.md](WHEEL.md).
2. **Roll d100** on that table, or
3. **read the table and pick** something you like, or
4. **make something up.** Always allowed, at every step, in every Age.

The tables exist to remove the blank page, not to decide the world. An entry you rolled and then ignored still did its job if it got you talking.

## Voice

These entries are not stat blocks. Nearly every one is a sentence with a turn in it — a fact plus a consequence, or a fact plus something unresolved:

> **The Stasis Core** — Time almost stops; things are preserved perfectly; nothing new can begin; nothing old can finish ending.

> **The Amber Well** — Time slows near things here; events are preserved in their immediate vicinity; the past is close to the surface.

Match that when adding entries. Each one should be usable as-is and should imply a problem. A table entry that only describes is doing half the work.

## Provenance

Converted from `.docx` with a parser that walks the Word XML table structure, so cell boundaries and the 100-entry counts are exact rather than reconstructed from flattened text. Five tables were split across page breaks in the source and were merged back. The source document is preserved in `_source/` (gitignored).
