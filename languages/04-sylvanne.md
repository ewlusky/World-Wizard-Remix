## Cultural Context

Sylvannë is spoken by the forest-dwelling elves of the Sylvan Covenants — long-lived scholars of nature, memory, and deep time. The language is deliberately beautiful, favoring flowing syllables, vowel harmony, and compound nouns of great precision. Sylvannë has no word for "enemy" — only "one who has not yet understood." Time in Sylvannë is expressed relative to seasons and tree-cycles, not years.

## Phonology & Grammar Rules

1. Vowel harmony: every word uses either front vowels (e, i, ë, ï) or back vowels (a, o, u, â). A word cannot mix front and back vowels. (Borrowed words from other languages break this rule and are marked as "outside-words.")

2. The letter ë is a soft "eh" sound (like the "e" in "bed"); ï is a sharp "ee" sound. Â is a long open "aah."

3. Consonants are mostly soft: l, n, m, v, s, th, r (lightly trilled). Hard stops (k, t, p) only appear at the start of words, never at the end.

4. Words end in a vowel or in -n, -l, or -m. No word may end in a stop consonant.

5. The definite article is na- (front harmony: ne-). Indefinite uses no article.

6. Possessives are formed as suffixes: -nni (my), -tva (your), -syl (their/its). A tree's canopy = sylvâtva (sylv-â-tva, the canopy-your of the forest).

7. Adjectives precede nouns and must harmonize in vowel class with them.

8. Verbs express time through prefixes tied to nature: an- (spring-new / beginning); sil- (summer-full / at peak); mel- (autumn-passing / declining); tel- (winter-still / completed).

## Phrases & Expressions

| Phrase | Literal Translation | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Lûma sylvnni | "The light of my forest" | Greeting to someone cherished / loved one |
| An-mirel thalvë | "I begin a new song from the root" | Starting fresh with integrity |
| Tel-annë câlân | "The memory is complete, like a star" | Said of the dead — their story is whole now |
| Ëlvas na mirna | "Wind in the sacred quiet" | A troublemaker disturbing peace / intrusion |
| Tûlian sylv-tva | "Your path through the forest" | How are you? / Where are you in your journey? |
| Nelvân ëlm brak | "There is no grief in forgetting a root" | Move on; not everything deserves memory |
| Sil-mirel sylvëna | "The elves sing at their fullness" | Everything is as it should be |

## Idioms & Cultural Sayings

| Idiom | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sylv lûma brak tûlian — "Forest light is not a path" | Beauty is not a guide. Just because something looks good doesn't mean you should follow it. |
| Annë câlân ëlvas — "Memory is a fixed star, not a passing wind" | True memory does not drift or change. Distinguishes fact from misremembering. |
| Thalvë mirna — "A root is silent" | Strength does not announce itself. Said admiringly of someone powerful who doesn't boast. |
| Nelvân-syl sylvëna — "The elves' grief is their own" | Said when someone's mourning is private and should not be pried into. |
| Tel-mirel lûmath — "The song is finished by dawn" | All things, even beautiful things, come to their end at the proper time. |
