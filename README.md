# World Wizard Remix

A collaborative, Age-structured worldbuilding game for 2–7 players. No GM. No preset world. Just a hex map, some index cards, and the accumulated weight of everything your group has made — and broken.

> *You are a Steward. Not a character. Not a Game Master. A caretaker of a world in progress.*

**Status:** Active design, pre-playtesting. Public as a design track, not as a finished game.

## Core concept

Players take turns spending Action Points to shape land, birth peoples, invoke magic, or complicate their own creations. The world grows across multiple Ages, each one closer in — a millennium is nothing in Age I; in the final Age, a season changes everything.

The map is a first-class game object. It accumulates weight. Things that fade leave marks. Languages drift and die. Ordinary people — the Kith — live and remember long after the gods stop watching.
